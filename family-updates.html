How these updates are written

Factual summary: a brief, neutral summary prepared from information supplied for this page. It is not written by Arjun and Erin, is not in Arjun and Erin’s voice, and does not express Arjun and Erin’s feelings. Every factual summary is labelled as one.

Family post: an entry written by Arjun and Erin and posted exactly as submitted — no wording added, edited, or paraphrased. Family posts are labelled as such, shown in a distinct style, and filterable separately, so the two are never confused.
