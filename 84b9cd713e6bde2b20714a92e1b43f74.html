Family post — posted verbatim

Written by the family themselves. Posted exactly as entered in the sheet — not edited, shortened, summarised, or reworded. These may be personal, because they are the family’s own words.

Sheet value: Entry type: Family post (verbatim)

To add a family post, add a row to the sheet, set Entry type to Family post (verbatim), and enter the full text in the What’s happening column. Only that text is shown, unchanged. The Findings & details, What’s next, When, and How to help columns are ignored for verbatim rows. Every other row is treated as a factual summary.

      How to read this page.

      Unless an entry is labelled “Family post — posted verbatim,” it is a factual summary. It records what happened, what was found, and what is scheduled. It does not add views, feelings, or wording on the family’s behalf.

      Information marked as outstanding was outstanding at the time the entry was written. Later entries record later information.

      Updates are listed newest first. When two updates share a date, the one added later in the sheet appears first; if a time is entered in the sheet’s Date column, it is shown with the date and orders updates within the day.

      Sharing news with friends or family?

      Please use the Family Updates page and share its link, rather than sharing this page. That page carries the version with less detail.
